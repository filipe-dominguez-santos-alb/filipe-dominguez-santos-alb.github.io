## The operator behind the terminal

This site is a personal knowledge base for cybersecurity, Linux, networking, automation, and experiments.

I believe in learning by building, documenting what works, and sharing notes that might help the next person.

### Ground rules

- Learn in systems you own or have explicit permission to test.
- Prefer reproducible notes over vague claims.
- Credit original research and link to primary sources.
- Stay curious. Stay ethical.
